# Martial and monster moves paintings — Session E

Fifteen source sheets for the RPG-SPELLS art brief, following movement-toolkit (Session A), auras & fields (Session B), healers & revives (Session C), and casters (Session D).
This batch is independent and adds no game code or simulation changes.

The 15 sheets:
- `issen` (steel, moonlight): razor-thin horizontal cut of white light across the field pointing right trail once + bright sliced flash with a crescent impact once (*Light*).
- `sneak-attack` (shadow with blood): quick dark backstab slash with a spray of red impact once.
- `flurry` (steel with gold): three rapid fist-shaped impact bursts overlapping impact once.
- `quivering-palm` (holy-white with blue): light gathering into an open palm shape cast once + resonant ripple bursting outward in rings impact once.
- `berserker-leap` (blood with earth): crater slam with red rage-fire and flying rocks on bottom edge impact once.
- `rage` (blood): red rage-aura of flame-like energy round a figure-sized space rising from bottom edge stand loop.
- `cleave` (fire-red with shadow): wide arcing sword cleave of dark-red hellfire impact once.
- `riposte` (steel): parry spark with two blades crossing, bright flash and counter-cut impact once (*Light*).
- `grappling-hook` (steel, rope brown): spinning iron hook pointing right travel loop + taut rope horizontal seamless beam loop + hook biting impact once.
- `dragon-dive` (fire with steel): figure-sized burst of flame and wind launching upward cast once + fiery ring blast on landing impact once.
- `seismic-slam` (earth): ground-pound shockwave of cracking earth and rock shards ring-shaped on bottom edge impact once.
- `pounce` (blood with steel): three raking claw marks with a spray impact once.
- `tentacles` (water, deep teal): dark sea-green tentacle rising from pool curling cast once + stretched tentacle with suckers along it seamless beam loop.
- `tidal-wave` (water): curling wave of sea water with foam pointing right rolling along the lane travel loop + crashing burst of spray impact once.
- `keg-blast` (fire with smoke): rolling powder keg with lit fuse pointing right travel loop + big black-powder explosion with fire, smoke and splinters impact once.

All sheets are structured as 256x256 px cells with an 8% clear margin on fully transparent backgrounds.
Validated against `python tools/spell-fx/import-spell-art.py art-source/spell-fx --check`.
