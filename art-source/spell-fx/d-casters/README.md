# Casters' spell paintings — Session D

Twelve source sheets for the RPG-SPELLS art brief, following movement-toolkit (Session A), auras & fields (Session B), and healers & revives (Session C).
This batch is independent and adds no game code or simulation changes.

The 12 sheets:
- `fireball` (fire): roaring bead of fire with short flame tail travel loop + big spherical fire explosion with shock ring impact once.
- `meteor-swarm` (fire with dark rock): flaming meteor of dark rock with long fire trail travel loop + crater blast with flying molten rock impact once.
- `magic-missile` (arcane): bright violet dart of force with crackling trail travel loop + crisp violet starburst impact once (*Light*).
- `eldritch-blast` (eldritch): seamless crackling beam of violet-black force with tiny eldritch runes beam loop + hollow burst of dark force with violet rim impact once.
- `drain-life` (eldritch with red): stream of red-violet life essence flowing left seamless beam loop + small red glow drawn out impact once.
- `circle-of-death` (eldritch): ring of necrotic black-green fire and skull runes ground loop (2:1 ellipse) + necrotic flare on foe impact once.
- `thunderwave` (lightning with wind): ring of thunder-force bursting outward along the ground crackling white-blue impact once.
- `time-stop` (arcane with gold): dome of frozen time closing with clock-hand shapes and cracks of light cast once (stands on bottom edge) + small frozen clock-sigil over stopped foe stand loop.
- `polymorph` (arcane, pink-violet): puff of pink smoke and sparkles impact once + small round cartoon fox shape in smoke cast once.
- `hunters-mark` (blood with gold): red crosshair rune with four arrow-points hovering and turning slowly stand loop.
- `bane-mark` (blood, dark): dark-red cracked crown rune hovering stand loop + iron chains clamping impact once.
- `mockery` (shadow-violet with gold): jagged speech-shape of mocking sound motes loop + sour burst on foe impact once.

All sheets are structured as 256x256 px cells with an 8% clear margin on fully transparent backgrounds.
Validated against `python tools/spell-fx/import-spell-art.py art-source/spell-fx --check`.
