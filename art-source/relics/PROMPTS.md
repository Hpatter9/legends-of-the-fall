# Relic paintings: prompts

Made by `tools/relics/paint-relics.py` (prompts from `tools/relics/relics.json`), cut out by `tools/relics/cutout.py`.
Every prompt is sent with one reference image for the style: the painted Ember Amulet (`art-source/gear/trinkets/emberAmulet.png`) on magenta.
Ids are the kebab-case of the new names; "old ids" are the relics each replaces (`docs/notes/relics-redesign.md`).

## The First Banner (`the-first-banner`)

Chapter I: The Founding · Common · old ids: banner

- not painted yet

> A single fantasy relic, painted as a game item icon in the same hand-painted style as the reference image (glossy, rich saturated colour, soft painted highlights, a crisp clean silhouette). Match only the painting style of the reference, not its object or colours. The relic is an ancient, treasured artefact, aged and worn: worn gilding, patina, small chips and scratches, old thread or wax where it fits, lit warmly as if by candlelight in a reliquary. A plain, well-used everyday relic. Colour accent: ivory-white and pale holy gold: a soft white-gold sheen on the metal and a faint warm white-gold glow on its edges. Subject: a tattered square war banner of faded cream cloth stitched together from several old cloaks, hanging from a short splintered wooden pole, embroidered with a green hill and nine small gold stars, frayed and slightly singed edges. Composition: exactly one object, centred, three-quarter view, filling about 80% of the square, not touching the edges. Background: a perfectly flat, solid, pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, one uniform colour everywhere, with no gradient, no vignette, no cast shadow, no floor and no glow spilling onto it. No text, no letters, no frame, no border, no hands, no scene. Square 1:1 image, PNG.
