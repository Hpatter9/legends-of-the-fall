Privacy Policy

Legends of the Last Keep · last updated 26 November 2026

Legends of the Last Keep is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. The version from Google Play offers optional in-app purchases; to process them, Google Play and our payment provider, RevenueCat, handle the purchase information described below. We do not sell your information, and we collect nothing else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (heroes, armies, settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Settings, Diagnostics keeps a short list of slow moments the game noticed, also only on your device; it leaves the device only if you choose to copy it and send it to someone yourself. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this.

Backups

On Android, if you have turned on backups for your Google account, Android may include the game's save in that backup so that it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account's settings; we never receive it.

In-app purchases (Google Play version)

Purchases are made through Google Play Billing: Google handles your payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see your card or bank details. To deliver what you bought and to restore it on a new phone, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • a random app user ID that RevenueCat makes for this install (not your name, email or Google account name);
  • your purchase history in the game: which items, when, the price and currency, and Google Play's purchase token and order ID;
  • basic device information its SDK sends with each request (the device model, the Android and app versions, the country of your store account) and, as with any web request, your IP address.

The Shop sells summoning stones, crowns, bundles and cosmetic looks (costumes, rulers and a summoning style). A cosmetic changes how the game looks, nothing else; Google Play's record of it, through RevenueCat, is what lets it come back after a fresh start or on a new phone.

This is used only to confirm and deliver purchases, to restore them, and to give us totals of sales. It is not used for advertising and is not sold. RevenueCat keeps it while your purchases may need restoring; you can ask us to have it deleted (contact below), after which purchases can no longer be restored.

Network use

The game plays fully offline. The version from Google Play connects to Google Play and RevenueCat only while you use the Shop and briefly at start (to deliver a purchase that did not finish and to keep bought cosmetics in step). (The version downloaded from the game's GitHub releases page has no Shop; it asks GitHub once at start whether a newer version exists; GitHub sees that request as it sees any web request, including your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. It sends nothing about you or your game. The web version at this address loads the game's own files from GitHub Pages, as any web page is loaded, and has no Shop either.)

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Apart from the purchase information above (only if a purchase is made), it collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
