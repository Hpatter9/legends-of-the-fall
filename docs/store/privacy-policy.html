Privacy Policy

Legends of the Last Keep · last updated [DATE]

Legends of the Last Keep is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your progress (heroes, armies, settings) is saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Settings, Diagnostics keeps a short list of slow moments the game noticed, also only on your device; it leaves the device only if you choose to copy it and send it to someone yourself. Uninstalling the game deletes all of this.

Backups

On Android, if you have turned on backups for your Google account, Android may include the game's save in that backup so that it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your account's settings; we never receive it.

Network use

The game plays fully offline. The version from Google Play makes no network requests. (The version downloaded from the game's GitHub releases page asks GitHub once at start whether a newer version exists; GitHub sees that request as it sees any web request, including your IP address, under GitHub's own privacy statement. It sends nothing about you or your game.)

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
