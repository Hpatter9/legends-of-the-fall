# Hero kits: the short version (COMBAT-002, built, ready for your review)

The full note is [`hero-kits.md`](hero-kits.md): what was built, where it differs from the design you approved, the balance before
and after, and the skills that still want their own painting. Screenshots are `docs/samples/hero-kits-*.png`.

## How it works, in plain words

- **Every hero has one pool.** Spell-casters (17 heroes) use **mana**, a blue pool of 100 that refills slowly, faster still while
  nobody is hitting them. Fighters (the other 28) use **stamina**, a gold pool of 60 that refills steadily and also when they are hit.
  A pool starts a fight a little short of a cast, so the first skills come once the fight is joined.
- **Two skills cost the pool.** Skill 1 from level 1, skill 2 from level 10 (and from level 10 the pool refills faster). The hero casts
  them on its own: heals go to the most hurt, blasts to the biggest crowd. Once both are open the two take turns, unless skill 2 has
  nothing to do.
- **The ultimate is yours.** It opens at level 30 and fills a ring round the hero's portrait as the hero deals, heals and takes damage:
  about 40 seconds of fighting (a front-liner about 30, a support about 50). When the ring is full the portrait in the new bar at the
  bottom of the fight glows gold and says TAP; tap it to fire. The **Auto** switch at the end of the bar (off unless you turn it on)
  fires them for you. Auto-replay always fires them. Each ultimate is at least as strong as it was.
- **The king's spells stay**, just above the hero bar, with their cards half as tall. The Last Stand and the Calamity have the bar too
  (two rows when there are more than five heroes).
- **Every old skill and ultimate stays**, with its painted effect and its tier 2 and 3 upgrades. Each hero gains one new skill; six
  ultimates gain a twist. The new skills borrow a recoloured painting for now; the list of those that want their own (FX-004) is in
  the full note.

## Ten of the reworked heroes

1. **Werewolf** shifts between a quick white hunting wolf whose bites bleed and a great black brute that shrugs off blows and hits
   everything round it. Full Moon is both at once.
2. **Druid** still calls her treant, and can *become* one for 8 seconds.
3. **Kitsune** takes the shape of the strongest foe on the field, with its looks, its reach and its attack.
4. **Beastmaster** calls wolves, then sets the whole pack on one foe.
5. **Reaper**: the foes he reaps rise as wraiths and fight for him a while.
6. **Mummy** binds the hardest-hitting foe and walks it into its own friends.
7. **Buccaneer** rolls two dice: a feeble cut on a bad roll, a triple-strength blow and haste for everyone on doubles.
8. **Time Witch** rewinds a badly hurt friend: its health and its place come back.
9. **Paladin** takes half the wounds of every friend near him on himself.
10. **Dread Pirate** rolls powder kegs that set off the battlefield's own powder barrels, one after another.
