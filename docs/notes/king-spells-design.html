Schools of the Crown

A revamp of the king's spells: what the system does now, what I found when I played and measured it, three directions, and the one I recommend.

Design only. No game code has changed. The same text is in docs/notes/king-spells-design.md.

1 · The system today

How the king's magic works on 0.331

PartHow it works now
Spells21. The starting three are Heal, Meteor and Haste. The rest: Shield, Smite, Chain Lightning, Poison Cloud, Frost Nova, Rally, Earthquake, Resurrect, Cleanse, Blink, Hex, Light Lance, Renewal, Deep Freeze, Battle Cry, Bulwark, Call the Wolves, Meteor Shower.
EarningThe Mage Tower teaches 12 (three a level). Six grand spells come from milestones. Gold chests hold a scroll 30% of the time, Mythic chests 60%.
ChoosingThe spellbook, opened by the Chapel (200 crowns). Three slots whose power adds up to 9, plus 1 at Mage Tower 2 and 4. The starting three cost 8.
ManaStarts at 2, fills 0.5 a second (×1.12 a Mage Tower level), holds 10.
CastingTap an orb then a tile (a 3×3 preview), or drag the orb to the grid. Four spells need no tile.
AutoThe one Auto switch casts spells by rule: help first, then harm, then buffs, with mana held back for the cheapest help spell. The game has been tuned for this player since 0.292.
The rulerEight rulers, a look and a voice. None changes a spell.

3 · Where it is weak

Nine problems

  1. The king is a heal button. With the starting three, Auto never cast Meteor (0.0 a fight in every cell). Alone, Meteor was never cast at the Labyrinth while the bar sat full for 55% of the fight. The king does a third to a half of all the healing.
  2. Collecting spells changes nothing. SPELLS-RETUNE found no set a top save can afford that beats the starting three. Eighteen spells are earned and none is needed.
  3. Few, small decisions. Fights of 10 to 45 s buy 2 to 6 casts. The power budget blocks pairs more than it invites them. A spell never grows.
  4. The numbers stay still. Meteor (110 raw), Smite (160), Chain (70), Lance (85) are flat against foes that grow with level and tier. Heal and Shield are shares of health, so they keep pace. Damage spells fall from 23% of the damage to 7–15%.
  5. Mana spills at the cap. Waiting for the right moment wastes mana at 10. A wave won early ends with a full bar.
  6. Fiddly on a phone. 40 px orbs (under 44 px), at the top of the screen, a hint over the board, and a 3×3 aimed on an isometric grid of moving fighters under a thumb. The same row holds the horn and Auto. A fight offers 3 spells, 5 ultimates, the horn, Auto and the speed.
  7. Overlap. Heal, Shield, Haste, Rally and Battle Cry repeat the Cleric, Knight, Paladin and bards. The Wizard's Fireball is a 3×3 fire burst like Meteor. The Eidolon horn is now the king's big moment and outshines every spell.
  8. The ruler is only a face. The Frost Witch carries "an annotated spellbook", the Necromancer "a spectral companion", and neither changes the magic.
  9. Auto helps well and harms timidly. Its rules suit Heal and Haste; harm spells wait for a crowd of three that seldom forms.
4 · Three directions

Ways to rebuild it

All three keep the sim deterministic, cast through the same castSpell, play offline and stay upright only.

A · The Royal Hand

A small deck you build and draw from.

Play
Build a deck of 8 cards from the spells you know (up to 2 copies). The hand holds 3; a cast card goes to the bottom and the next is drawn. The cap rises to 12, and a card held 12 s can be swiped away for 2 mana.
Phone
Three 56 px cards just above the hero rows, where the thumb is. Drag one up onto the board.
Progression
Duplicate scrolls rank a card up (1 to 3). The Mage Tower raises the deck limit to 10, then 12.
Auto
The chooser reads the hand, with a rule to cycle cards it will not use soon.
Balance
Each card's worth per mana set against one yardstick, then every mode re-measured with the bot on 8-card decks.
Art and sound
Card frames per element, a card back, draw and discard sounds. Spell effects reused.
Trade-off
Every spell can earn a place and each fight takes a new shape. But it adds luck to a game the player can barely steer, and the ruler is still only a face.
Recommended

B · Schools of the Crown

Each of the eight rulers leads a school of five spells and one Decree.

Gilded Necromancer · DeathResurrect, Raise Dead, Drain Life, Hex, Bone Wall
The Gilded Toll
Decrees in italics. Most spells are today's 21 moved to the school that suits them; about 14 are new.
Play
Three slots from your ruler's school and one open slot for any spell you know from another school. No power budget. Mana that would spill over 10 fills the Decree meter instead (a little more comes from each hero ultimate). Full, the ruler's face glows and one tap casts it, once a fight. The horn stays the Eidolon's.
Phone
Four 52 px orbs in one row, the Decree as the ruler's face at the end. Tap then tile, or drag, with a soft snap that puts the 3×3 on the nearest crowd under the finger. The hint shrinks to a label on the orb.
Progression
Three ranks per spell, bought with school marks from the Mage Tower and the school's own milestones. Rank 3 adds a rider: Meteor leaves the ground burning; Frost Nova shatters the frozen.
Auto
A rule per school (freeze before Shatter; Raise Dead where foes fell). The Decree when a boss is below half or three heroes are. A looser crowd rule (two foes, or one elite) so harm spells get cast.
Balance
Damage spells scale with level and tier like hero skills. Each school is measured so none falls more than 10% behind the starting set in tries to clear. Then the armies, tiers, daily, Last Stand and Calamity are re-measured to the owner's pace, turning only the knobs that move.
Art and sound
Eight Decree effects and about 14 new spell effects from the Pixel Effects atlas and the packs. The rulers' command pose, a voiced Decree line each, about 28 new sounds, school badges.
Trade-off
The ruler matters, the starting three stop being the answer, Auto uses harm spells, and no mana is lost. It is the biggest build, and it turns the ruler from a look into a play choice.

C · Royal Momentum

Spells that charge and grow during a fight.

Play
Two spells, no mana. Each spell's ring charges from the fight (hits, kills, ultimates) to level 1, 2 or 3; a tap casts it at its level. Two elements in a row make a combo: Frost then Meteor shatters, Poison then Fire explodes.
Phone
Two 64 px rings at the bottom right, under the thumb. No aim: harm lands on the thickest crowd, help on the most hurt.
Progression
Levels raise each spell's top charge and open combos in a combo book.
Auto
Casts at level 2, or waits for 3 by a setting.
Balance
Charge per hit, one value per mode, then the usual re-measure.
Art and sound
Three looks per spell, combo flashes, a rising charge sound.
Trade-off
The easiest on a phone, with rising tension in every fight. But no aim means less control, combos need many pairs tested, and it copies the heroes' own charge meters.
5 · Recommendation

Build B, with two pieces of C

Schools of the Crown, plus the Decree meter fed by spilt mana and the soft snap for aiming. It gives the ruler its own magic, lets spells rank up and keep pace with the game, gets Auto to use harm spells, and gives the king a moment that is not the horn's.

Four fixes that stand alone

  1. Bigger orbs (48 px or more) and the hint moved off the board.
  2. Damage spells scaled to level and tier, not flat numbers.
  3. Auto's crowd rule eased so it casts Meteor and the other area spells.
  4. Mana over the cap kept, in a meter or a rising cap.

Build order if B is approved

  1. The four fixes and the measure.
  2. Schools and the slot rule on today's 21 spells, the Decree meter and two Decrees.
  3. Ranks and school marks.
  4. The new spells, school by school.
  5. The other Decrees, voices and sounds.
  6. The full re-measure to the owner's pace.
