# INT-326: the repainted Eidolon and quest art (PR #326)

Branch `claude/int-326`: the owner's `art/eidolons-1b` (14 source PNGs under `art-source/eidolons/`, a repaint that replaces PR #238)
with `origin/main` merged in. Same sizes as the files they replace, so the layouts did not change.

## How they came in

`tools/eidolons/import.py` (the Eidolon art pipeline, `docs/notes/eidolons.md`) re-ran from the sources:

- **`horn/` (4) and `call/` (2 of 3)** -> `src/art/eidolons/horn-{empty,light,ready,blast}.webp`, `eid-circle.webp`, `eid-rift.webp`.
  The horn is a new painting (a bound horn on a leather strap, gold when lit); the four states still share one box so the
  stacked layers line up. `call/eid-motes.png` is not read by the importer (the cinematic draws its motes itself) and stays a source.
  The six Eidolons' figures and cards are untouched (the importer rewrote them identically, nothing changed in git).
- **`givers/` (5) and `board/notice.png`** -> `src/art/quests/{gwen,pedlar,pilgrim,messenger,hunter}-face.png` and `notice-face.png`
  (128 px square, new `givers()` step in the same importer). They replace the pack placeholders under the keys `src/questArt.ts` keeps
  for them (`FACES`; `notice` now shows the pinned paper; `patron` and `teal` follow Gwen's and the pilgrim's). The quest board in the
  tavern (`BOARD`) and Gwen's bar sprite (`GWEN`) are still the pack art: a bust is not a sprite.
  `board/notice-sealed.png` is not used yet (kept as a source).

## Small code changes the paintings needed

- `src/questArt.ts`: the new `notice-face.png`, and `PAINTED` (the keys whose face is a painting).
- `src/questCard.ts`, `src/quests.css`: a painted face is drawn smooth (`.qFace img.painted { image-rendering: auto }`); the pixel
  placeholders stay crisp. Without this, the 128 px paintings shrunk to 40 px with nearest-neighbour looked jagged.
- `CREDITS.md`: the repaint is credited as original art made for this game with an image model (no model name).
- `tools/quests/cut.py` still cuts the other placeholders; do not re-run it for the five faces above (the importer owns them now).
