# Ilyra's guardian figure: a ChatGPT prompt

ULT-AUDIT (`docs/notes/ult-audit.md`): Ilyra's Death Denied wants a figure of light that catches each ally. The Blender pipeline is
good at light, rings, fire and shards and poor at figures (a figure modelled of plain solids was tried and taken out), so the figure
is painted art. Until it lands her strike is wings of light and a halo on each ally.
