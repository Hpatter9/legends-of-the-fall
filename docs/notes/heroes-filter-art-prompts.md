# Art prompts: the roster's Sort and Filter medallions (HEROES-FILTER)

The roster's Sort and Filter buttons (`docs/notes/heroes-filter.md`) wear stand-ins from the painted button kit: the down-chevron
glyph (`g-down`) for Sort and the Auto-pick medallion (`auto-pick`) for Filter. Two medallions of their own would read better. They
follow the kit's medallions (`docs/notes/painted-buttons.md`): a brass ring with a picture, cut to `src/art/ui/buttons/<name>.webp`
by `tools/painted-buttons/cut.py`.

Repo `hpatter9/legends-of-the-fall`, branch `art/heroes-filter`.

## Sort medallion: `art-source/ui/buttons/sort.png`

```
A single round fantasy RPG button medallion for the game "Legends of the Last Keep", painted in a rich hand-painted style with soft
rim light and gentle texture, like a premium mobile RPG interface icon. A polished brass ring with small rivets round a dark
slate-green field. In the field: three horizontal gold bars stacked one over another, the top the longest and the bottom the
shortest, with a small gold arrow pointing down beside them, meaning "sort". Warm brass and gold, dark steel, deep green, as in a
gothic medieval keep. The medallion alone, centred, front view, filling most of the square, on a fully transparent background, with no
text, no shadow on the ground and nothing else in the picture. 256 x 256 pixels, transparent PNG. It must read clearly at 28 pixels.
```
