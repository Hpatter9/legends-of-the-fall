# int-330: castle rooms art

Brought in PR #330 (`art-source/castle/`: the smithy, the grand watchtower, the Hall of Legends and 17 room icons; original art made with an image model) and ticked off every line of `depth-art-todo.md`.

- `tools/castle/import-room-art.py` does the cutting. The three buildings are 2x the castle pictures, so each is halved by a 2x2 box average, as `prep.py` does the kit pieces: `src/art/castle/smithy.png` (95x110), `watchtower_grand.png` (79x134), `hall_of_legends.png` (119x112). These are exactly the stand-ins' sizes, so the cards keep their layout. `SPRITE_SIZE` in `castleSpots.ts` lists them.
- Smithy and Hall of Legends cards use their own picture (`edificeUrl` in `castle.ts`). `lookPicture` gives the grand watchtower at the Watchtower's top level (3); levels 1 and 2 keep the kit tower.
- Room icons: `Room.icon` in `src/sim/rooms.ts` is now a name (`anvil`, `thread`, `mark`, `books`, `scroll`, `quill`, `candle`, `cross`, `eye`, `moon`, `compass`, `crossed-swords`, `plinth`, `lectern`), so the sim file stays free of the page. `src/roomIcons.ts` maps each to a 48 px WebP in `src/art/rooms/`, bundled into game.js (no service-worker entry is needed). The four seats share the plinth. `forge`, `shield` and `star` were not needed and stay in `art-source/`.
- Tests: `castleScene.test.ts` now counts the three card-only pictures (`CARD_SPRITES`) as used, since they show on cards, not in the yard; the size check against the files is unchanged. `rooms.test.ts` checks every room's icon has a file and every file is a room's icon.
- Shots: `docs/samples/int-330/` (castle page and the three cards, at 393x852, 360x780, 412x915), from `tools/castle/room-art-shots.mjs`.
