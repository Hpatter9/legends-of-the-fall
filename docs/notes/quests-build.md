# Quests 3: the build (running notes)

Building the owner-approved [quests-design.md](quests-design.md), in its nine batches (Q1 to Q9). The owner's answers: both the stones and
the Eidolons harder; about 3,000 stones in year one; the cut on the chest shop and the spire's re-climbs; stars are difficulty only, with
length a separate tag; ★1 and ★2 quests pay no stones; the 149 existing quests hardened (quests already taken keep their old goals);
real-day time limits from ★3 with a cooldown on failure; 3 quests plus 1 campaign slot; army shard drops unchanged.

## Q1: stars and length

- `src/sim/questStars.ts`: `STAR_TABLE` (an authored `[stars, length]` for every quest), `starsOf`, `lengthOf`, `starEstimate` (the
  weights: a goal's size, each rider, the tier asked, a quest battle's weight), `needLevel`/`openLevel`/`comfortLevel` (the opening
  level comes from the armies' recommended levels; the comfortable level adds 0/0/5/10/15 for ★1 to ★5), `starLine` ("★★★☆☆ · Long · Lv 45+").
  `QuestDef` gains optional `stars` and `length`, which win over the table (so a new quest can carry its own).
- The table was generated by `npm run quest-stars table` from the estimate and a story's length (2 to 4 linked parts: Chain; 5 or more:
  Campaign; big counts and dailies: Long), then tuned by hand: the two tutorial quests are ★1, the quest battles ★3 (Karnok's Door ★4),
  the hero tales and lost techniques ★4 Chain (the design's call; they are set by their battles, so a test exempts them from the
  estimate check).
- Shown on: the board's rows, the Active and Done rows, the giver's card (offer and going), and the tracked chip (as ★n). The board sorts
  easiest first (a chip flips it) and filters by length.
- `npm run quest-stars` prints the spread (stars by length) and the quests that stray from the estimate.
- Screenshots: `docs/samples/quest-stars-*-{393,360,412}.png` (`node tools/quest-stars/shots.mjs`).

**What is not done in Q1 (said plainly):**

- **The save-like probe.** The design asked for a probe with talents, gear and relics before calibrating stars on measured win rates.
  This batch rates quests by the estimate and the design's own star assignments for battles and tales; it does not play fights. The
  probe is left to Q4, which retunes the quests and the battles' `ease` and needs the measurements.
- **The spread test** (every stage of a save has three star counts and two lengths open) is not asserted yet. Today's 149 quests are the
  easy ones the owner complained of, so the table is mostly ★1 to ★2 with no ★5. The spread becomes a test in Q4 once the retune and the
  new quests exist.
