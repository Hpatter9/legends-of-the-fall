# Quests 3: the build (running notes)

Building the owner-approved [quests-design.md](quests-design.md), in its nine batches (Q1 to Q9). The owner's answers: both the stones and
the Eidolons harder; about 3,000 stones in year one; the cut on the chest shop and the spire's re-climbs; stars are difficulty only, with
length a separate tag; ★1 and ★2 quests pay no stones; the 149 existing quests hardened (quests already taken keep their old goals);
real-day time limits from ★3 with a cooldown on failure; 3 quests plus 1 campaign slot; army shard drops unchanged.

## Q1: stars and length

- `src/sim/questStars.ts`: `STAR_TABLE` (an authored `[stars, length]` for every quest), `starsOf`, `lengthOf`, `starEstimate` (the
  weights: a goal's size, each rider, the tier asked, a quest battle's weight), `needLevel`/`openLevel`/`comfortLevel` (the opening
  level comes from the armies' recommended levels; the comfortable level adds 0/0/5/10/15 for ★1 to ★5), `starLine` ("★★★☆☆ · Long · Lv 45+").
  `QuestDef` gains optional `stars` and `length`, which win over the table (so a new quest can carry its own).
- The table was generated by `npm run quest-stars table` from the estimate and a story's length (2 to 4 linked parts: Chain; 5 or more:
  Campaign; big counts and dailies: Long), then tuned by hand: the two tutorial quests are ★1, the quest battles ★3 (Karnok's Door ★4),
  the hero tales and lost techniques ★4 Chain (the design's call; they are set by their battles, so a test exempts them from the
  estimate check).
- Shown on: the board's rows, the Active and Done rows, the giver's card (offer and going), and the tracked chip (as ★n). The board sorts
  easiest first (a chip flips it) and filters by length.
- `npm run quest-stars` prints the spread (stars by length) and the quests that stray from the estimate.
- Screenshots: `docs/samples/quest-stars-*-{393,360,412}.png` (`node tools/quest-stars/shots.mjs`).

**What is not done in Q1 (said plainly):**

- **The save-like probe.** The design asked for a probe with talents, gear and relics before calibrating stars on measured win rates.
  This batch rates quests by the estimate and the design's own star assignments for battles and tales; it does not play fights. The
  probe is left to Q4, which retunes the quests and the battles' `ease` and needs the measurements.
- **The spread test** (every stage of a save has three star counts and two lengths open) is not asserted yet. Today's 149 quests are the
  easy ones the owner complained of, so the table is mostly ★1 to ★2 with no ★5. The spread becomes a test in Q4 once the retune and the
  new quests exist.

## Q2: the stone cut

- **Shop** (`chestShop.ts`): bronze 1, silver 3, gold 6 stones (prices, stock and gear unchanged).
- **Daily chest**: 1 stone and one a bonus star (`DAILY_CHEST_STONES`, was 2). **Calamity**: 1 stone a draw (`CAL_STONES_PER_DRAW`, was 3), and the
  gold chests of a clear hold half their stones, rounded up (`StashChest.half`).
- **Last Stand (and the old climb)**: chests of a run that does not pass the record are `dry` (`StashChest.dry`, no stones; crowns, tomes and gear as
  before). A new best (`run.best > m.stand`; the old climb: `floors > prevBest`) holds stones as before. Both flags are read safely and are optional,
  so old saves load as they were.
- **Quest pay** (`questPay.ts`): `questPay(stars, length)`, `rewardOf(q)` and `shareOf`. The stones are the design's table; a one- or two-star quest
  pays none (a two-star chain's total is 1), and its chest is `dry`. Crowns are the table's (150, 300, 600, 1,000, 1,600 for a Short; times 1.5, 2, 3)
  but never less than the quest already paid. A story's total (a chain or a campaign) is split over its parts, the finale taking the most. Hero shards
  from quests are for four stars and five only. `handIn` pays `rewardOf`, and the log and the cards show it.
- **Pace tool** (`npm run unlock-pace`): now prints a per-source ledger of the year's stones, models quests from the pay table (`--noquests 1` leaves
  them out) and the new spire and Calamity rules.

Measured (10 seeds, a daily player): about **2,680 stones in a year** (design: about 3,000). By source: shop 1,355, daily chest 508, quests 287, Calamity
clear and chest 267, spire 158, achievements and codex 59, army first-clear and star chests 45. The pullable heroes owned come on days: 5th 2, 10th 25,
15th 78, 20th 147, 30th 257, 35th **337** (design: 302). So the cut lands a little deeper than the design's target (about 10% fewer stones). The
first session still reaches five heroes for about half the saves on day 1 (seven stones) and nearly all by day 3. If the owner wants the design's
day 300 exactly, the daily chest's base stone or the shop's silver chest (3 to 4) is the lever.

Tests: `test/quest-pay.test.ts` (new); `summon-stones.test.ts` and `quests.test.ts` updated to the owner-approved numbers (the shop's 12 stones a day, the first
day's seven stones, a one-star quest paying crowns only).

## Q3: the new goal rules

All in `quests.ts` and `questData.ts`; every field is optional, so old saves load as they were.

| Rule | Where | Fact it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Several objectives (`goals`, with `steps` words for the checklist) | `objectivesOf`, `countOf`, `needOf`; `QuestActive.more` holds the counts after the first | none new |
| In order (`ordered`) | an objective counts only once the one before was done before the fight | none |
| One run (`oneRun`) | `QuestActive.runId`; a different run, or a run ending undone (`QuestFight.runOver`), starts the counts again | the run's seed, `run.over`/`cleared` |
| No hearts (`noHeart`) | `condMet` on `QuestFight.hearts` | `run.retries` |
| No hires (`noHires`) | on `QuestFight.hires` | `run.summons` |
| No ultimates (`noUlts`) | on the fight's `ults` | already counted |
| Level cap, best benched, heroes barred, exactly n | `QuestFight.levels` and `best` (`bestHero`: the highest level, first owned among equals), the fielded heroes | levels from `levelOfKind` |
| Time limit (`within`) | `tickQuests(m, date)`, called by `refreshOffers`: past its days the quest leaves the log, `Meta.quests.failed[id]` holds the date, and `canOffer` waits `FAIL_COOLDOWN` (3) days | the date the page passes in; never the clock |
| Streak (`streak`) | one count a day; `QuestActive.streakDay`; a missed day starts again (at the next count, or by `tickQuests`) | the settle date (`noteQuests(m, f, date)`, default the day the board was last drawn) |
| 3 quests and 1 campaign | `CAMPAIGN_MAX`; `cannotTake`, `readQuests` and the log's "Active 2/3 + 1/1" | `lengthOf(q) === 'campaign'` |
| `questPlace(m, q, a)` | `questGoto.ts`: the first unfinished objective's place (the existing "Take me there", `destinationOf`, now reads it), and `ruleLines`: the rules in words, shown on the Active row | |

- `withQuests(extra, fn)` (questData) lets tests and tools run with extra quests known to `questById`.
- A goal open to any army with a tier asked now points "Take me there" at the furthest army that has the tier open.
- The log: a checklist for several objectives (✓/○ with counts), the rules as an italic line, an amber "N days left" chip (red on the last day) and, in Done, a struck-through "Failed: back on the board in N days".

**Not done in Q3 (said plainly):** the party screen does not show the rule line ("No ultimates in this fight") and Auto-pick does not yet
respect a level cap, a barred hero or a benched best (the rules are counted correctly either way; the player sees them on the Active row).
No quest uses the new rules yet (the retune is Q4), so the screenshots of the checklist and the limit chip come with Q4.
Tests: `test/quest-rules.test.ts` (new, 10 tests).

## Q4: the retune (mechanism)

`src/sim/questRetune.ts` holds `RETUNE`, a record by quest id of `Retune`s (new stars, length, goal, riders, words); `questData.ts` lays each over its
quest, keeping the old goal and text as `was`. A quest taken since (`QuestActive.v = 1`) counts the present goal; one taken before, or in a save
from before, has no `v` and counts `was` until it is handed in or dropped (`defFor`, `activeDef`). The pay follows the new stars at hand-in.
