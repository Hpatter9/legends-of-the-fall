# Bloodtusk art correction

Owner approved all four art corrections after reviewing PR #495. Work is on `art/bloodtusk-weathering`, based on the warcamp branch; scope is Bloodtusk artwork, its rendering and validation.

Two original transparent sheets replace the stand-ins: dedicated smith, woodcutter and butcher poses; four healthy timber variants; one unfinished siege engine; and low shale, roots, footings and doorstep details. Sources are retained in `art-source/assault/orc/`. Actual generation briefs and the older separate-sheet briefs are recorded in [the art prompts](bloodtusk-weathering-art-prompts.md). Art credits are in `CREDITS.md`.

`tools/assault/pack-warcamp.py` measures the painted rows, trims transparent padding, scales the art and carries foot anchors into the existing Orc atlas. It appends a bounded strip, can be rerun without accumulating copies, and refuses to overwrite an unknown atlas extension. After recutting the original Orc sheets, rerun this packer. The original atlas pixels and damage-state sprites remain intact. The new atlas uses the existing URL; `build.mjs` copies and precaches it as before, without a new runtime dependency.

Workers use four deliberate work keyframes with a resting pose between cycles. Each job has its own art; reduced motion holds the resting pose. Existing proximity/alarm/occupied-tile rules still hide them before combat. They remain outside the simulation. The repair engine keeps a 2×2 solid footprint and its burn state. Visual review found that the elevated hall hid the original yard from the fixed camera. The engine, spare mechanism and nearby timber were moved to the west-side work lane (engine u30–31, v46–47), clear of the hall silhouette; all map-access checks were repeated and passed. Only healthy palisades choose a weathered variant: damaged, broken and breached pieces retain their actual state pictures. Low foundation aprons overlap existing solid footprints; door wear only appears on open level ground. Cliff-edge art is baked as low decoration and creates no obstacles.

Validation: typecheck and web build passed. Nine affected test files passed (looks, warcamp, map, renderer, duel, siege, mirror, resume and ambient); looks passed again after adding artwork-specific coverage. After relocating the yard, seven affected files passed again, followed by typecheck and build. Built atlas matches the packed source byte-for-byte, and the existing service-worker inventory includes its URL. Fully loaded final views were reviewed at 393×852, 360×780 and 412×915, plus forge, butcher, lumber worker, ridge, relocated repair yard and reduced-motion close-ups; no browser errors. The forge reports two decorative workers; reduced motion reports zero smoke with static workers. All final overviews retain 235 simulation actors and complete chunk loading. An initial navigation timeout was resolved with an explicit longer load timeout. Captures use the software-rendered main-thread fallback and are visual evidence, not device performance measurements.
