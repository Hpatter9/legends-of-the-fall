# SIM-001: effective hits and environmental credit

Issue: #121. Owner: Codex implementation chat. Branch: `codex/sim-001`.
Base: `6837fe7f4b40ab73c25a8d5b1b3234fee8c98cdd`, version 0.157.0.

## Reproduction and change

Before implementation, the first 15 focused regression cases produced 12 failures
and 3 passes. Dead, dodged and fully shielded targets incorrectly allowed leech
and equipment hit counters; terrain deaths incorrectly recorded self damage as
contribution. This was confirmed by running the bundled tests directly with Node
(the nested repository runner reports file totals rather than individual cases).

`src/sim/battle.ts`: `hurt` now returns whether damage reached health. `settle`
only calls `afterBlow` on success, including lethal hits and hits followed by
revival. Dead targets, dodges and fully absorbed shields return false. Existing
nominal blow damage scaling for leech, equipment, through and arc is retained;
partially absorbed hits still qualify. On-strike charge, defensive equipment,
thorns, ordinary splash, death events, shield handling and revival keep their
existing order and mechanics.

Environmental damage uses the victim as its source. Such self-hits no longer
increment `dealt`, `kills` or `slain`, or invoke passive/equipment kill hooks.
The rule applies to standing lava, lava entry and barrel chains through their
shared damage function. It does not redirect barrel credit to the instigator.
Damage events retain their existing IDs and source, so death/win settlement
continues normally. Genuine attackers, including damage over time, retain credit.

`src/sim/fightStats.ts`: self-hit events still add damage taken but cannot add
damage dealt or kills, including when a summoned unit maps to its caller's row.
This is a pure statistics change; no UI module changes.

`test/sim-001.test.ts`: 19 focused cases cover dead/dodged/shielded targets for
leech, equipment hit counts, through and arc; partial/exact shields; terrain
deaths; genuine kill hooks; revival; and cancellation of a dead melee wielder's
queued strike. `docs/TASKS.md` records bounded ownership and the handoff.

## XP and progression

Lasting `fightXp` and local `fightXpByHero` consume contribution counters; terrain
now adds neither damage XP nor slain XP. Participation and victory XP remain.
Focused tests check both lasting and local settlement after a terrain death.
Summon contributions continue to roll up to their caller. Last Stand separately
rewards foe deaths with shared gold/XP, regardless of killer: that existing rule
is retained; environmental deaths are still emitted as deaths. No save schema or
keys change, no new randomness is introduced, and no version bump is made.
