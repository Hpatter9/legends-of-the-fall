# Trait perks: art prompts

Three pieces of art the trait perks need that neither the packs nor `src/art/` have (`docs/notes/trait-perks.md`, "Art and effects").
Each goes to `hpatter9/legends-of-the-fall` on the branch `art/trait-perks`, under `art-source/trait-perks/`. Until they land, the fight
uses the stand-ins the design names.

## The Machine's sentry turret (Sentry Network)

```text
Pixel-art sprite sheet for Legends of the Last Keep, a dark-fantasy auto-battler seen from a three-quarter isometric view (FF Tactics style).
Output art-source/trait-perks/sentry_turret.png, 768 × 384, transparent background, no text. A small clockwork sentry turret, about the
height of a kneeling soldier: a brass and dark-iron tripod, a rotating drum housing with one stubby rifled barrel, rivets, a glowing amber
lens, steam vents. Two rows of six 128 × 192 frames: row 1 unfolding from a folded crate into the turret (frames 1 to 6, the last its idle
pose); row 2 firing (a recoil, a muzzle flash, a puff of steam, back to idle). Facing down-right. Crisp pixel edges, a limited palette of
brass, iron and amber with dark outlines, matching the game's robot units (rifle bot, siege bot). Even, soft top-left light.
```

## The Pirate's ghost ship (Broadside)

```text
Pixel-art sprite for Legends of the Last Keep, a dark-fantasy auto-battler. Output art-source/trait-perks/ghost_ship.png, 1024 × 512,
transparent background, no text. A spectral pirate galleon seen side-on, broadside facing the viewer: torn glowing sails, a pale green-teal
ghost light, wisps trailing off the hull, a row of eight gun ports. Two frames side by side, 512 × 512 each: frame 1 the ship at rest,
frame 2 every gun port flashing with fire and smoke. The ship shows at the battlefield's edge as its cannons fire, so keep it readable as a
silhouette, slightly translucent. Crisp pixel edges, a limited palette of teal, bone-white and ember orange with dark outlines.
```

## The Royal crown mark (The Crown)

```text
Pixel-art icon for Legends of the Last Keep, a dark-fantasy auto-battler. Output art-source/trait-perks/crown_mark.png, 128 × 64,
transparent background, no text. A small floating gold crown that hovers above a hero's head to show who wears the Royal crown: five points,
a red gem in the middle, a bright rim light. Two frames side by side, 64 × 64 each: the crown, and the same with a brighter glint on its
points (for a slow shine). Crisp pixel edges, gold, crimson and a dark outline, readable at 24 pixels tall on a phone.
```
