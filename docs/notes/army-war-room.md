# Army War Room and Party Strength

Owner: Codex. Branch: `feature/army-war-room`; worktree: `/workspace/legends-of-the-fall`. The owner chose the War Room from three visual concepts, asked for party-based stretch suggestions and Party Strength, and authorised implementation and a PR. No merge, deployment or version bump.

The Armies carousel is now a candlelit campaign room with three selectable army banners, using the game's existing painted boss keepsakes. A new background painting leaves the live labels and controls in the DOM. The selected army has an ornate briefing well, Party Strength, the current challenge estimate, tier choices, a suggested available tier, rewards and Prepare Battle. The action and reward summary precede the longer scrollable stars, conditions, ore and Assault details. Locked armies use sealed keepsakes; existing tier gates, replay tickets, continuation/restart confirmations, Assault and tribute handlers are retained. Off-screen army cards are inert for keyboard access. Reduced motion uses immediate carousel navigation and the painting has no animation loop.

Party Strength is the existing `partyPower` sum of `kindPower`, including level, rank, equipment and talents. It is derived on every draw, with no new save field. The party builder uses the same label for every company; army preparation also shows a live challenge estimate and target as the draft changes. Generic editing and quest companies clear the campaign context.

`src/sim/armyChallenge.ts` contains deterministic presentation advice. Normal's target is the mean starting hero power at the army's existing recommended level, multiplied by the company's available slot count (three early on, at most five). Higher tiers scale that target by the ratio of their strongest wave's summed foe power to Normal's, reading the actual wave boosts, boss health/attack multipliers, speed and extra escorts. A strength/target ratio of at least 1.3 is Comfortable, 0.9 Matched, 0.7 Stretch, otherwise Overmatched. The suggested unlocked tier is closest to a 0.8 ratio among viable tiers; if all are Overmatched, it suggests the lowest unlocked tier. It may be Comfortable or Overmatched when no available tier is a stretch; it never unlocks content or changes the selected tier without a tap.

This is a heuristic, not a simulated win probability. Formation, bonds, spells, castle bonuses, ultimates and tactical decisions can change the result. The UI labels it an estimate. No balance, deterministic fight rules, rewards or save schema changed.
