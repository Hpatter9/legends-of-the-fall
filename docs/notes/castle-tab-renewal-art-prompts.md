# Castle tab layout concept

Repository: `Hpatter9/legends-of-the-fall`. Design branch: `design/castle-tab-renewal`; art branch for future production paintings: `art/castle-tab-renewal`.
Source: `art-source/castle-tab-renewal/layout-concept.png`. Canvas: 1536×1024, opaque (transparent background: no). Proposal only; not a production background or implemented screenshot.

```text
Create a polished mobile fantasy game UI concept sheet showing TWO portrait phone screens side by side, each the same scale, left THRONE HALL and right BUILDINGS, for Legends of the Last Keep. Detailed hand-painted dark medieval fantasy style with aged brass trim, dark green and slate panels, warm gold readable text. Compact readable hierarchy, restrained decorative framing, generous intentional spacing. Each screen includes the same compact top currency row (crowns 1200, purple crystal 25), header The Keep with Castle rank 11/48 and small Codex and Trophies utilities, and two internal tabs Throne Hall / Buildings with the corresponding view selected. Left screen: a prominent deep stone throne hall with cool stained glass, warm braziers, crimson carpet, one seated crowned armoured ruler, four small guards and a white goat; short ruler caption Iron Stag; below, one construction panel Barracks upgrading, Level 2 to 3, 14:22 remaining; below a calm compact 2x3 Castle bonuses summary +20 Gold, +15% Attack, +6% Health, +12% Mana, +4% Speed, +6% XP using small medallions, no giant individual gold frames. Right screen: same header and tabs, compact active construction strip, then readable full-width building rows with small painted building thumbnail on left, name and level, benefit beneath, clear right-side chevron/action status: Treasury Lv 2/5 +20 starting gold Upgrade 180; Barracks Lv 2/7 +12% health Building 14:22; Armoury Lv 3/7 +15% attack Upgrade 240; Mage Tower Lv 1/4 +12% mana Upgrade 180; Stable Lv 1/5 +4% movement Upgrade 150; Library Unbuilt +6% XP Build 150. These rows are the management view, no courtyard map or floating labels. Both screens have existing bottom navigation Recruit, Heroes, Battle, Castle, Spells with Castle highlighted. Show all screen edges, no device hardware frame, no hand, no fake notification bars. Tiny label above the sheet Castle tab layout concept. This is an aspirational UI mockup, not a screenshot of implemented game.
```


# Painted building thumbnails

Repository: `Hpatter9/legends-of-the-fall`; branch: `art/castle-tab-renewal`; source: `art-source/castle-tab-renewal/building-scenes.png`. Canvas: 1536×1024. Background: opaque, not transparent. Packed as twelve separate rectangular thumbnails, retaining the source.

```text
Paint a production asset sheet of exactly TWELVE separate medieval fantasy building illustrations arranged in a seamless FOUR COLUMNS by THREE ROWS equal rectangular grid. Canvas 1536x1024. Each of the twelve tiles is an independent 384x341 scenic painting, full bleed, no borders, no gaps, no letters, no labels, no UI. Consistent hand-painted detailed dark fantasy game art, aged stone and timber, deep green banners, slate roofs, warm golden window light against cool blue dusk, crisp readable silhouettes. Every building entirely inside its own tile, no object crosses a tile edge. Row 1 left to right: (1) Treasury, secure grand stone vault with golden arched door and a few coins in foreground; (2) Barracks, fortified soldiers' quarters with red banners and practice swords; (3) Armoury, weapon workshop with crossed swords and shield racks; (4) Mage Tower, slender wizard's tower with restrained violet magical windows. Row 2: (5) Stable, timber stables with a horse at an open stall; (6) Library, scholarly stone building with books visible through warm arched windows; (7) Chapel, small gothic sanctuary with a shining stained glass window; (8) Smithy, blacksmith forge with warm fire, anvil and chimney smoke. Row 3: (9) Watchtower, tall lookout tower above a fortified wall with green banner; (10) Hall of Legends, majestic hall with hero statues flanking its doors; (11) Drill Yard, open parade training ground with wooden practice dummies and a sergeant pavilion; (12) Construction, stone building foundations with timber scaffolding and tools. Match refined painted fantasy tavern and royal hall style, NOT pixel art, NOT flat icons, NOT a combined courtyard. Exactly 4x3 independent paintings, no text.
```
