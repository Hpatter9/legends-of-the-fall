# INT-386: PR #386 ("a uniform fortress gothic pass") reviewed and brought onto the standard

Branch `claude/int-386`: origin/main (0.373.0) with `uniform_menu_popup_ui` merged in by a merge commit, then the integration on top.

## The review

PR #386 restyles ten menus and pop-ups (the Auto card, the talent box, the fight's unit card, the tome sheet, the relic card, a ware's
sheet at Hester's Stall, "How the altar works", a finished build's card, the Upgrades confirm and the full artwork viewer), and changes
three dismiss words ("‹ Back" and "Back" to "Close" on the Auto card and the talent box, "×" to "Close" on the unit card).

- **Not the standard builder.** None of the ten is built with `ornateMenu`. The pass is about 450 lines of CSS appended to
  `ornateMenus.css`, nearly every declaration `!important`, on each dialog's own classes. That is the hand-drawn card the standard forbids,
  and the allow-list in `test/ui-standard.test.ts` did not shrink.
- **It duplicates section 24.** The card it paints ten times over (the double brass rim, the slate gradient over `ui/page.png`,
  `--ornate-shadow`, `#0f130ecc` wells with `--ornate-well`, `#6b5c40` choice rows going `#f2dc9f` / `#444c38`–`#293021` when chosen) is
  `.om`, `.omWell` and `.omChoice` copied out. It is also numbered "24" beside the existing 24, and it repeats main's own section 8 rules
  for `.tModal` / `.tBox`.
- **UX.** What it changes is right: the three dismiss words (cards say Close) and 44 px taps (main's Auto counts were 28 px, the unit
  card's × 32 px). Text at 11 px or more: it fixes "BUILT" and the relic card's rarity, though not the relic's 9 px piece names.
- **Motion.** It adds no animation; reduced motion is unaffected. No flashing.
- **Offline.** No new URL assets (`ui/page.png` is already in the service worker's FILES).
- **Checks.** At the merge commit `npm run typecheck` and `npm run web` pass.

The look is good, since it is the standard look. So the dialogs were moved onto the builder rather than kept as overrides.

## What was taken

Built with `ornateMenu` now (crest, eyebrow, title, filigree rule, wells and footer from section 24):

- **The Auto card** (`autoAsk.ts`): eyebrow "Repeat runs", the four counts as a row of gilded choices (`menuSection(..., 4)`, a new
  four-across grid in the standard), "Auto runs left today" as a note, Close and "Auto-fight ×3" in the footer.
- **How the altar works** (`altarHelp.ts`): eyebrow "The Rift Altar", the five lines in a well, "Got it". It covers the page now, not
  only the altar card. Escape counts as seen.
- **A finished build** (`builtCard.ts`): eyebrow "Built", the building and its level in the header, its picture and what it gives in a
  well, and Close. A tap anywhere still closes it. Its CSS-in-a-string is gone.
- **The tome sheet** (`tomeView.ts`): eyebrow the hero's name. "Done" becomes Close (a card says Close). The level-up flourish is
  unchanged.
- **The relic card** (`relicCard.ts`): eyebrow "Relic", the name as the title, the card's body on the menu's slate. A tap anywhere still
  closes it. The frame no longer takes the rarity's colour, but the rarity label keeps it.
- **The talent box and its Auto-spend confirm** (`talentView.ts`): the tier and cost as the eyebrow, the picture and the rule in a well,
  Close or Learn. The confirm keeps "Cancel" (a word of its own) and Confirm.
- **A ware's sheet at Hester's Stall** (`stallView.ts`): a standard sheet (`kind: 'sheet'`). The eyebrow is "Hester's Stall" or "A
  trader's ware", the art is centred, a gear piece's stats are in a well, and the footer is Close and "Buy · 500" (the reason it cannot be
  bought is a note above).

Kept as plain rules (section 26 of `ornateMenus.css`, no `!important`): the unit card's "Close" at 44 px; the artwork viewer on
`--ornate-border-double` and `--ornate-shadow`, with "× Close" now "Close".

Removed: the PR's whole section, and the dead CSS of the old shells (`.autoAsk`, `.tModal`, `.tBox`, `.tTitle`, `.tBtns`, `.tAutoHead`,
`.tomeSheet`, `.tomeCard`, `.tomeClose`, `.rlWrap`, `.rlClose`, `.altarHelp` and `.altarHelpBox`, `.hsVeil`, `.hsSheet`, `.hsSheetTop`,
`.hsShut`, `hsUp`, and main's section 8 `.tModal` / `.tBox` rules). The relic shot tools now look for `#relicCard`.

## What was left out, and why

- **The unit card** (`unitInfo.ts`) stays its own. It docks above the fight's bottom panel (`--fs-dock`), shares `fhSheet` with the HUD's
  sheets, and leaves the board visible. A standard veil would blur the fight under it. Only the Close word and its 44 px are taken.
- **The Upgrades confirm** (`.ulAsk`) is a well inside the Upgrades list (`rankUpList.ts`, still on the list), not a dialog of its own.
  It moves when the list does. Main's version already meets the taps.
- **The full artwork viewer** is a native `<dialog>` (focus kept inside it, Escape, an inert page) holding a full-height picture. It is a
  viewer, not a menu. The rim token is taken, the rest is not.
- **The PR's per-dialog `!important` colours and fonts**, as above: they copy section 24.

## The allow-list

`BEFORE_THE_STANDARD` loses four: `altarHelp.ts`, `autoAsk.ts`, `builtCard.ts` and `tomeView.ts` (17 to 13). `stallView.ts` stays, because
the stall page is still its own dialog. `relicCard.ts` and `talentView.ts` were never on the list (they set no `role="dialog"`); they get
one now through the builder. A new test checks that each moved dialog is built with `ornateMenu`.
